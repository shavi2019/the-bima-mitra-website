6. Insurance Act Compliance | बीमा अधिनियम अनुपालन

We operate in full compliance with:

  • Insurance Act, 1938: Regulates insurance business in India
  • Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority Act, 1999: Establishes IRDAI
  • Insurance Brokers (General Insurance) Regulations, 2017: Governs insurance brokers
  • Insurance (Amendment) Act, 2015: Recent amendments to insurance laws
  • IRDAI Consumer Protection Rules, 2020: Protection for insurance consumers
