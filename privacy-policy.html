🔒 Privacy Policy | गोपनीयता नीति

Last Updated | अंतिम अपडेट: 26 July 2024

1. Introduction | परिचय

The Bima Mitra ("Company," "We," or "Us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.thebimamitra.com (the "Site") and use our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Site or Services.

2. Information We Collect | हम जो जानकारी एकत्र करते हैं

A. Personal Information Provided by You:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, postal address
  • Identity Information: Government-issued ID, Aadhaar number, PAN
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, income information, investment history
  • Health Information: Medical history (for health insurance), pre-existing conditions
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle registration, insurance history, driving records
  • Profile Information: Age, occupation, lifestyle habits, family details

B. Information Collected Automatically:

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date
  • Cookies: Preferences, tracking information, authentication tokens
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, mobile network information
  • Behavioral Data: Clicks, scrolls, form submissions, time spent on pages

C. Information from Third Parties:

  • Insurance companies (for policy verification)
  • Credit agencies (for credit verification)
  • Government agencies (for KYC verification)

3. How We Use Your Information | हम आपकी जानकारी का उपयोग कैसे करते हैं

The Bima Mitra uses collected information for various purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide insurance advisory services and policy assistance
  • Customer Support: To respond to inquiries and resolve complaints
  • Product Recommendation: To suggest suitable insurance products based on needs
  • Account Management: To maintain user accounts and facilitate transactions
  • Compliance: To meet KYC, AML, and regulatory requirements
  • Communication: To send promotional emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages (with consent)
  • Website Improvement: To enhance user experience and optimize website functionality
  • Analytics: To track usage patterns and improve our services
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent activities
  • Legal Compliance: To meet legal obligations and regulatory requirements

4. Sharing of Information | जानकारी साझा करना

We may share your information with:

  • Insurance Companies: To process your insurance applications and claims
  • Financial Institutions: For KYC verification and credit checks
  • Government Authorities: As required by law (IRDA, IT Department, etc.)
  • Service Providers: Email services, payment gateways, hosting providers
  • Legal Representatives: In case of disputes or legal proceedings

We DO NOT:

  • ✗ Sell your personal information to third parties
  • ✗ Share information without your consent (except as required by law)
  • ✗ Use information for purposes other than those mentioned
  • ✗ Retain information longer than necessary

5. Data Security | डेटा सुरक्षा

Security Measures We Implement:

  • Encryption: 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Access Control: Restricted access with role-based permissions
  • Authentication: Two-factor authentication for admin access
  • Regular Audits: Quarterly security audits and penetration testing
  • Firewalls: Advanced firewall systems and intrusion detection
  • Backup: Regular encrypted backups with disaster recovery
  • Employee Training: Data security training for all employees

Note: While we implement robust security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We recommend you use strong passwords and keep your login credentials confidential.

6. Cookies Policy | कुकीज़ नीति

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Authenticate your login sessions
  • Track website usage patterns (analytics)
  • Personalize content and recommendations
  • Measure advertising effectiveness

Types of Cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality
  • Preference Cookies: Store your preferences (language, theme)
  • Analytics Cookies: Track how you use the website (Google Analytics)
  • Marketing Cookies: Used for targeted advertising

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may limit website functionality.

7. Your Privacy Rights | आपके गोपनीयता अधिकार

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request and obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your information (where applicable)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your information
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in portable format
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Right to Lodge Complaint: File complaint with data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@thebimamitra.com with proof of identity.

8. Data Retention | डेटा अवधारण

We retain your personal information for the duration necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Maintain business and transaction records

Retention Periods:

  • Active Users: Information retained while account is active + 5 years
  • Inactive Users: Retained for 3 years after last transaction
  • Legal Disputes: Retained until dispute resolution
  • Compliance Records: Retained as per IRDA and IT Act requirements (7-10 years)

9. Third-Party Links & Services | तीसरे पक्ष की लिंकें

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (insurance companies, payment gateways, social media, etc.). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy applies only to our website. When you visit third-party sites, their privacy policies govern their data collection practices.

We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Children's Privacy | बच्चों की गोपनीयता

Our services are not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy | नीति में परिवर्तन

The Bima Mitra reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to the website.

We will notify you of significant changes via email or prominent notice on the website.

Your continued use of the website constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
